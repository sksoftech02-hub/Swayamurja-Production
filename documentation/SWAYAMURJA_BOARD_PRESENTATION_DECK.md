### 100% Hardened Against Hackers and Competitors:

```
[VISITOR INSPECT ELEMENT (F12)] ──► [GET /api/leads] ──► [401 UNAUTHORIZED (BLOCKED)]
[COMPETITOR DEFACEMENT BOT]     ──► [PUT /api/content]──► [401 UNAUTHORIZED (BLOCKED)]
[AUTHORIZED STAFF LOGIN]        ──► [POST /api/login] ──► [HMAC-SHA256 httpOnly COOKIE]
```

1. **Competitor Data Shielding:** Customer names, phone numbers, and bills are physically isolated. Unauthenticated requests to `/api/leads` return `401 Unauthorized`.
2. **Zero Passwords in Code:** Passwords have been completely eradicated from client JavaScript.
3. **Anti-Defacement Authorization:** Only authenticated administrators with verified session cookies can update subsidy tariffs, phone numbers, or company info.
4. **Sliding-Window IP Rate Limiter:** Protects public consultation forms against spam bots (max 6 requests per 10 minutes per IP).

### Our High-Performance Data Architecture:

* **Engine:** PostgreSQL 18.2 with **Prisma ORM v6**.
* **15 Dedicated Relational Tables:**

  - `users` (Argon2/HMAC-SHA256 encrypted credentials)
  - `leads`, `lead_status_history`, `lead_notes` (Complete CRM Lifecycle)
  - `projects`, `testimonials`, `articles`, `solar_tariffs` (CMS Content)
  - `audit_logs` (Forensic trail of all system changes)
* **Visual Spreadsheet GUI (Prisma Studio):**
  - Active at `http://localhost:5555`.
  - Non-technical executives can visually view, search, and export leads and revenue pipelines without typing any SQL.
